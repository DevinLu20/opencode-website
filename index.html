Cheap Eats Boston

Boston · student budget

Good food, student prices.

Ten spots where a real meal still costs less than a textbook. Filter them by what you can spend and what you can eat — no accounts, no delivery apps, no ten-dollar fees.

Prices are ballpark ranges for a single person, not quotes. Menus and hours change, so check before you make the trip.

Three ways to eat

A rough guide to what your money buys. The tiers below match the filter buttons further down the page.

Under $10

Grab and go

Lunch between classes or dinner on the walk home. Counter service, short menus, and food that travels well.

Look for: pizza slices, falafel wraps, cannoli.

$10–20

Sit down or take out

The everyday tier: a burrito, a bowl of pho, or a proper plate of vegetables with enough left for coffee.

Look for: burritos, noodle bowls, veggie plates.

$20–30

Treat yourself

For a birthday, a visit from home, or the end of finals. Share plates with friends to keep the bill near the bottom of the range.

Look for: small plates, pasta, dishes for the table.

  • Gene's Chinese Flatbread Cafe

    $6–9

    Chinatown · cash only

    Hand-pulled noodles and cumin lamb flatbread. Tiny room, fast turnover, and a line that looks worse than it is.

    • Vegetarian options

  • Mike's Pastry

    $5–8

    North End · cash preferred

    Cannoli and a coffee can count as dinner if you commit to it. Grab a box for the group and skip the sit-down places on Hanover Street.

    • Vegetarian

  • Falafel King

    $7–10

    Downtown Crossing · counter service

    A falafel wrap with everything, plus free pickles while you wait. The lunch rush is real, so go at 11:30 or after 2.

    • Vegetarian
    • Vegan

  • Anna's Taqueria

    $10–14

    Allston · counter service

    Burritos the size of your forearm. Rice bowls and vegetarian options are on the board; ask them to hold the dairy if you need it dairy-free.

    • Vegetarian
    • Gluten-free options

  • El Jefe's Taqueria

    $10–15

    Kenmore Square · open late

    Burritos, bowls, and tacos with no upcharge for guac or queso. Vegan chicken, vegetarian fillings, and gluten-free bowls are all on the board, and the doors stay open until 3 a.m.

    • Vegetarian
    • Vegan
    • Gluten-free options

  • Tasty Burger

    $10–15

    Fenway · late-night window

    A veggie burger and fries near the ballpark without the ballpark markup. Loud, bright, and open when the dining halls are not.

    • Vegetarian

  • Raising Cane's

    $10–16

    BU / Allston · 949 Commonwealth Ave

    Chicken fingers, crinkle-cut fries, and Texas toast. The Box Combo is the sweet spot; there is no meatless main, but the fries, toast, and coleslaw are vegetarian.

    • Vegetarian sides

  • Clover Food Lab

    $10–18

    Back Bay · counter service

    Chickpea fritter sandwiches and seasonal plates. Order at the counter; most of the menu is vegan and the staff know the ingredients well.

    • Vegetarian
    • Vegan
    • Gluten-free options

  • Myers + Chang

    $20–28

    South End · dinner

    Shareable Taiwanese-leaning small plates. Go with two friends, split three dishes, and the bill lands near the bottom of the range.

    • Vegetarian
    • Gluten-free options

  • Coppa

    $22–30

    South End · enoteca

    Pastas and small plates in a snug room. Sit at the bar, order two plates, and call it a celebration without the tasting-menu price.

    • Vegetarian
